# Who else could solve the customer's problem?

Public supplier pages reviewed on 23 November 2026. The comparison identifies products a buyer could consider and a commercial model from a neighbouring market. It uses published offerings. Actual shortlists, contract prices and buying decisions require interviews.

## Alwayse's starting point

Alwayse already offers design expertise, installation support and bespoke movement equipment. Its air-cargo range includes ball units for transfer decks, models designed to let debris escape and flanged units that can be changed quickly. The company also sells through a global distributor network. A lifecycle pilot could build on these capabilities by assigning responsibility for the readiness of a specific deck zone after installation. Scope, payment and the distributor's role would need agreement. ## An adjacent service model

Jungheinrich UK's Power Buy The Hour keeps a forklift on site for a monthly standing fee, charges for hours used and includes full service. This gives interviewers a concrete way to discuss sharing fixed readiness costs and variable usage costs. A passive transfer deck has no inherent operating-hours meter. A per-movement pilot would need an agreed count from the handler, defined exclusions, and a reconciliation process. A fixed fee per zone is a simpler option to test when those records are unavailable. [UK hire offer](https://www.jungheinrich.co.uk/products/forklift-hire/power-buy-the-hour-flexible-contracts-based-on-hourly-usage-144016)

## First commercial experiment

1. Ask the cargo handler, maintenance provider and distributor to reconstruct one recent deck-zone interruption. Identify who logged it, who held the replacement, who could approve the work and who bore the delay cost.
2. Compare the current arrangement with two proposals for the same zone. One supplies a replenished spares kit and scheduled review. The other adds a response commitment during agreed cover hours. Ask the economic buyer which budget could pay and what evidence would justify renewal.
3. Before offering availability credits, agree how the customer measures scheduled operating time, which failures the supplier can control, and who judges disputed incidents. Record an explicit decision to narrow or stop the offer if the buyer cannot verify the outcome or the provider cannot cover its costs.

The pilot and interview plan are candidate proposals. The suppliers above have not supplied prices or endorsed the proposed terms.
